I will present this accepted technical paper during **IEEE Quantum Week 2026**, held at the **Metro Toronto Convention Centre** from **13 to 18 September 2026**. The precise session date and time should be treated as pending until the final technical-paper programme is published.

**Authors:** Léa Cassé, Sabarikirishwaran Ponnambalam, Gregory Pearson, Nick Lim, Bernhard Pfahringer, and Albert Bifet.

The paper is part of the **Quantum End-to-End Hybrid Case Studies (QECS)** technical-paper track and presents an end-to-end QRU–CVaR–QUBO–QAOA workflow for spatial flood-risk allocation.
